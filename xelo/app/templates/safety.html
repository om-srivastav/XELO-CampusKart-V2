LOOK OUT FOR EACH OTHER

Good finds.
Safe exchanges.

Meet on familiar ground.

Choose a busy, well-lit campus location during the day. Tell a friend where you are going, or bring them along.

Take a closer look.

Check condition and test the item before agreeing to buy. Ask for proof of ownership for valuable electronics or bikes.

Keep personal details personal.

Never share OTPs, passwords, identity documents or banking login details. You do not need to enter a PIN or scan a payment code to receive money.

You set the pace.

Avoid advance payments to someone you don't know. XELO connects students; it does not process payments, hold escrow, guarantee items or verify payment receipts.

Something feels off?

Use Report listing, Report user or the report link on a message. Moderators can review reports and restrict accounts. For immediate danger, contact campus security or local emergency services.

Your privacy controls

Phone and WhatsApp sharing are off by default. You can change them in your profile. Turning on WhatsApp reveals your number to members who follow that contact link. Internal messaging works without revealing a phone number.
